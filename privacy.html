How your data is handled

Bildagboken is a private app for keeping track of a car together. It has no ads, does not sell any data and does not track what you do.

What is stored

  • Your account: the name you enter, your email address and how you sign in (Apple or Google).
  • The car and the log: what you enter yourselves, for example fill-ups, service, odometer readings, costs, places, bookings, the to-do list, photos and documents.
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, an address for your phone is stored so that reminders can be sent to it.

Everything is stored in Google's cloud service Firebase. Only the people who share the car can see it. Settings such as appearance and the order of the cards are stored only on your phone.

Services the app uses

  • Google Firebase for sign-in, storage and notifications.
  • Apple Maps to search for garages and places and to show maps. What you search for is sent to Apple.
  • OpenStreetMap and Photon as a fallback when Apple Maps cannot find an address or a place.
  • Registreringsnummer API (registreringsnummerapi.se) for the car's details. When you add a car, the registration number is sent there, and the service answers with, for example, the make, model, model year, colour and VIN. Nothing about you is sent, and owner details are not stored.
  • Wikipedia for a picture of the car model, if you have not added your own.
  • Anthropic (Claude) for reading with AI in Bildagboken Plus. When you choose Read with AI, the photo of the receipt, invoice or protocol is sent to Anthropic, which reads it and sends the details back. When you ask Glenn a question (Ask Glenn), the question and what you have logged about the car (car details, maintenance, costs, driving log totals and the log, but no photos) are sent to Anthropic, which answers. Anthropic does not use the images or the details to train its models.
  • Your location when you ask Glenn, if you have allowed it. The approximate location (coordinates and nearest address) is sent with the question so that Glenn can answer about the weather and find the nearest petrol station or workshop. For that, the coordinates are passed on to OpenStreetMap (the search) and Open-Meteo (the weather), without anything about you. The location is not saved. The app remembers roughly where you were (to within a kilometre or so) on the phone and asks Open-Meteo for the weather there, so that Glenn can dress for the weather.
  • Google (Text-to-Speech) for Glenn's voice in Bildagboken Plus. When an answer is read aloud, its text is sent to Google, which turns it into speech.
  • Apple (speech recognition) when you talk to Glenn in the iPhone app. What you say is turned into text by Apple. The audio is not saved in Bildagboken.

Without Plus, receipts are read on your phone, and the image is not sent anywhere to be read.

The app asks for your location when you tap the pin button, to suggest the nearest petrol station or garage. That location is not stored.

In the driving log (Plus), where a trip starts and ends, and the addresses, are saved with the trip in your household. With the automatic driving log in the iPhone app, the phone measures trips with GPS even when the app is closed. The route stays on the phone until the app is opened, and then only the start, end, distance and times are saved. You can turn it off at any time.

Shared service book

If you share the service book with a buyer, anyone with the link can see it: the car's details and the history of service and maintenance. You can close the link at any time under Car.

Help and support

The person who runs Bildagboken can see the data in order to help if something has gone wrong, and to see how many people use the app and which features are used. Nothing is changed unless you have asked for it.

If you have questions about your data, or want something removed or corrected, email kontakt@bildagboken.com. You can also delete your account yourself, see below.

Delete your data

Under Settings → Account → Delete my account your account is deleted right away. If you are alone in the household, the cars and the whole log are deleted too. If there are more of you, what you have logged remains for the others.

You can also download everything as a file under Settings → Your data.
